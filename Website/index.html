Please, allow us to brag!

Every component is handmade with not love but pain, suffering, anger, confusion and a now huge ego hehe.

The so called BEANS are connected to the computer via an Arduino DUE R3-E running a C++ script. BEAN ONE contains eight buttons and two potentiometers soldered to a perfboard. BEAN TWO is made up of membrane switches, recycled from a charity shopbought bathroom floormat.

All containers, button caps, pot caps and the like have been 3D modelled in Onshape and then printed.

We have decided to call our collection of lights, Trailer Trash Electronics.
Bumfights is an assortment of LEDs soldered to a perfboard and connected to an Arduino R4 Minima, also running a C++ script, chocking, we know.
Crackhead, as you can see, is our most handmade looking item. Standing proudly on a pandora box with it's old IKEA desk lamp light and snusdosa lid stuck to a motor taken from a game controller with it's weights ripped off.
